05

Motion

One easing — “thread” cubic-bezier(.2,.75,.1,1). Fast 180ms for feedback, 420ms for state, 760ms for reveals. Reduced motion removes all of it.

Rise

Text and blocks lift 28px and unmask on entering view. 70ms stagger.

Weave

The hero net: a WebGL lattice that lifts toward the cursor and ripples on tap.

Draw

Signatures and connectors trace their stroke — used for Sign and the platform diagram.

Glide

The one horizontal moment: tools slide sideways, pinned to vertical scroll.

Stack

Hero cards shuffle forward; the left copy crossfades to match.
